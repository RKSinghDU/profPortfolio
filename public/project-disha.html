The premise

A company that keeps going after you decide

GreenLeaf Retail's payroll works. Almost everything else in HR runs on spreadsheets and instinct. A new CHRO has won a ₹6 crore mandate from a sceptical Board — and chartered your pod to spend it. Nothing resets between rounds: a shortcut taken in Round 2 returns in Round 5, with interest.

Decisions are earned, not clicked

Options unlock only after you analyse the data. Your rationale must cite the figures you computed — an unevidenced choice scores zero on rigour, however sensible it was.

Five patterns are hidden in the data

The workbook has analytical patterns planted inside it. Nobody tells you what they are or where they sit. Finding them — and proving it with numbers — is the work.

Same option, different marks

Two pods can choose identically and score very differently. The choice is a third of it. The analysis behind it, and the risks you admit, are the rest.

Why we teach this way

Simulation-based learning at the Department of Commerce

A case asks you to explain a decision someone else already made, with the outcome printed at the end. Disha removes that safety net.

One standing warning

In any round, a live situation at GreenLeaf may land the moment you press Submit — and your option and rationale lock at that instant. Respond with data you have already checked, not with reassurance.

Three scarce currencies

You cannot buy your way out of this

Every option is affordable alone. None of them are affordable together.

Budget ₹

₹6 crore for the whole programme. Overspending is penalised — so is underspending that starves adoption and leaves a good system unused.

Change Capital

Organisational trust. Disruption burns it; honest communication earns it back. Let it fall far enough and resistance inside GreenLeaf intensifies.

Analyst Days

Your analytical capacity — granted once, for the entire programme. It does not refill each round. Spend depth where the round rewards it.

And the ledger

Deferred problems are recorded

Anything you postpone is written into a visible debt ledger and raises the cost of the round where it finally has to be fixed. Deferring is legitimate. Forgetting is not.

Private · ungraded · yours

The pod is marked.
You are mirrored.

Alongside every round you file your own private submissions — seen by nobody on your team. At the end of the specialisation those submissions are drawn into an HR Kundali: a six-instinct chart of the kind of HR professional you are becoming. It carries no marks and never appears on a leaderboard. It is returned to you, once, as a keepsake.

01 · DIAGNOSTICIAN

Evidence-led

Establish what is true before acting.

02 · HUMANIST

03 · ARCHITECT

04 · COACH

05 · DEVELOPER

Growth-oriented

06 · OPERATOR

Decisive-investor

Commit resources fast and contain.

Your strongest instinct is your archetype; your weakest is your blindspot. Both are named when the chart comes back to you — and the blindspot is usually the more useful conversation.

Nobody else sees it

Private between you and me. Not shown to your pod, not ranked against classmates, not published anywhere.

Two rings, never merged

How you saw yourself is drawn separately from how others saw you, and from what you protected under pressure. The question is whether the shapes match — not whether the numbers do.

You cannot game it

How each submission scores is never published, for exactly this reason. Answer as you actually think, and the chart is worth having.

Before you ask

Four things worth knowing now

Do I need to be good at statistics?

You need to be willing to open a spreadsheet and compute a mean, a rate, a ratio and a cross-tab. Everything past that is taught in the round where you first need it.

What if my pod disagrees on a decision?

Good. The pod submits one option, but your private reflection asks directly where you hesitated or saw an alternative. Dissent is recorded, not penalised — and it is exactly the kind of thing your HR Kundali is built from.

Can we catch up if we miss a round?

No. Locking is sequential and there is no catch-up mechanism — a pod that misses a round cannot open any round after it. If something goes wrong technically, contact me before the deadline, not after.

Can I use AI tools?

To understand the dataset and structure an argument, yes. To invent figures you claim to have computed, no — any cited number can be checked against the workbook in seconds.
