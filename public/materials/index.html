How to save the DSEH004 slides as a PDF

DSEH001 lectures are ready-made PDFs: click Download PDF on any card. For DSEH004:

  1. Click Save as PDF on the lecture card. The slides open and your browser's Print window appears.
  2. Next to Destination or Printer, choose Save as PDF.
  3. Press Save. All slides are saved, one slide per page.

On a phone, open the slides and use your browser's Share button, then Print, to save a PDF.
